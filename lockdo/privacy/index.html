Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

LockDo turns the screenshots you take into reminders you confirm. Your to-dos never leave your phone: there is no LockDo account and no LockDo server. After the free trial, LockDo can show one optional Google AdMob rewarded ad a day to free users; that is the only reason the app uses the internet.

The short version

  • Your reminders, tasks, screenshots, recordings and the text read from them stay on your phone. They are never sent to us or to an ad network.
  • Screenshot reading and text recognition run on your phone.
  • After the 7-day trial, a free user may choose to watch one short rewarded ad to use LockDo free for that day. Ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some device and advertising data (below). There are no ads for Pro users, during the trial, or for children, and never banners or ads in the middle of a task.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Information on your phone

The app stores what you create or import (reminders, tasks, Drops, titles, dates, checklists, tags, images, files, audio, recognised text and the contact or group details you pick), plus your settings, your ad-day status and your purchase status. All of this stays in the app’s private storage on your phone and is used only to run the app.

Where your phone offers on-device language features, LockDo may use them to suggest summaries and share-card drafts. LockDo does not send your prompts, screenshots or recordings to any cloud text-recognition or AI service. Automatic results can be wrong; review them.

Advertising

After the 7-day free trial, a free user sees a calm screen offering to watch one short ad to use LockDo free until local midnight. You choose whether to watch. Without the ad, reading, search, export and existing reminders keep working; reminders never go silent.

  • Who provides the ads: Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The ad software is started only when you choose to watch an ad. It is never started for Pro users or during the trial.
  • What the ad software collects: as Google describes it, device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or, if you allow tracking, the IDFA (iPhone); your IP address and a coarse location derived from it; device and app information; how you interact with the ad; and diagnostic data. Google uses this to show, limit, measure and protect ads under its own policies.
  • What it never gets: your reminders, tasks, notes, screenshots, recordings or recognised text. That content does not leave your phone on any plan.
  • Personalised ads and your choice: ads may be personalised. On iPhone, the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears only the first time you choose to watch an ad, never at launch. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choice first. If you refuse tracking or consent, you get non-personalised ads, which Google still uses limited data for (for example to cap how often an ad is shown and to prevent fraud). You can change your choice in your phone’s settings (iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) and, where offered, from LockDo’s settings.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch an ad (see above).
  • Things you share. Exports, share cards, Packs, Pacts and calendar events go only where you send them (for example WhatsApp, Mail or your storage provider). Their policies then apply.
  • Device sync and backups are encrypted files you move yourself; they are not a LockDo cloud account. Backups are passphrase-encrypted by default. We never hold your passphrase.
  • Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include app data, depending on your settings.
  • Apple or Google handle downloads and purchases under their own policies.
  • Write to us opens a draft in your own mail app with only what you type plus the app version and your phone’s system version. If you send it, we receive the email and your address.

Purchases

LockDo Pro removes ads and unlocks Pro features. Apple or Google handle the payment, account details and transaction under their own policies. LockDo reads only product and entitlement information to unlock Pro and restore it.

Deleting your data

Your content stays until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the library on that phone. Exports, phone backups, calendar events and copies you sent to others stay where they are and must be deleted there. Because we hold none of your content, we cannot delete it for you. To reset Google’s advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Children

LockDo is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect children’s data. Ads are not shown to children.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you at amakatsolutions@gmail.com. We hold nothing from the app other than any email you send us. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version ships, and change the effective date above.
