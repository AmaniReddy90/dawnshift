Contact us

Email amakatsolutions@gmail.com. In the app, More → Settings → Write to us opens a draft in your own mail app with the app version and your phone’s system version, which you can read and change before sending. Please do not send private screenshots or passphrases.

Common questions

Does LockDo upload my screenshots or to-dos?

No. Text is read off a screenshot on your phone. There is no LockDo account and no LockDo server. Your to-dos never leave your phone unless you share them.

Why does LockDo show an ad?

After the 7-day trial, free users can watch one short rewarded ad to use LockDo free for the rest of the day (until local midnight). It is your choice; Pro removes ads entirely. There are no banners and no ads in the middle of a task. The ad comes from Google AdMob and never receives your reminders or screenshots.

Why did my iPhone ask to allow tracking?

The tracking prompt appears only the first time you choose to watch an ad. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still get the ad day, with non-personalised ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks first.

What still works if I do not watch an ad or buy Pro?

Reading, search, export and existing reminders keep working, and your reminders never go silent. Creating new items needs an ad day or Pro. Nothing is deleted.

A reminder did not arrive. What should I check?

Open More → Settings → Reminder delivery. It shows what your phone allows (notifications and, on Android, exact alarms and battery optimisation) and what to change. Focus or Do Not Disturb can also hold notifications.

How do I move to a new phone?

More → Settings → Export a backup writes an encrypted file; open it with LockDo on the new phone. Keep the passphrase safe: we never have it and cannot recover it.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or uninstall LockDo to remove everything on that phone. We hold no copy. To reset ad data, reset your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. See the Privacy Policy.

Pro, cancelling and restoring

Restore: More → Settings → Restore Purchase, with the same store account. Cancel a subscription on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
