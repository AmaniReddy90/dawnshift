Common questions

Do you see my documents?

No. Scanning, reading, search and Ask happen on your phone. There is no account and no DawnShift server that receives your files. Files leave your phone only when you share or save them, or if you switch on Apple’s private cloud for Ask.

What is “Use Apple’s private cloud for harder questions”?

An optional setting in Settings → Ask on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, harder questions and the matching passages may go to Apple Private Cloud Compute, and those answers are labelled. Turn it off at any time.

Why does Ask show passages instead of a written answer?

Written answers need an on-device model: Apple Intelligence on iPhone, or Gemini Nano or the optional “Smart answers” download on Android. Without one, or if a quote in the answer cannot be found in your document, Ask shows the best matching passages.

How do I search files I already have?

Tap the search bar, choose “Add a folder” and pick a folder. Your phone asks once. To stop, tap “Stop using folder”: the search entries are deleted, your files are not touched.

A file is missing from search.

Older .doc, .xls and .ppt files and password-protected Office files cannot be read. Reading may still be in progress; the search screen shows how many files it has looked at.

Can I password-protect a PDF?

No. Only the batch ZIP export can be locked with a password you choose. We cannot recover a forgotten password.

Is there advertising or tracking?

No ads, no analytics, no account.

Deleting your data

Delete documents in the library, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on that phone. We hold no copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Pro, cancelling and restoring

Where Pro is offered, it is managed by the store. Cancel on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Use “Restore purchases” with the same store account on a new phone. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
