Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

Docwise lets you scan, read, search and ask questions about your documents. It is built so that your documents stay on your phone. There is no Docwise account and no DawnShift server that receives your documents.

The short version

  • Scanning, text recognition, search, automatic titles and folders, key facts and Ask run on your phone.
  • Docwise contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and there is no sign-in.
  • Your documents leave your phone only when you choose to share, save, export or print them, or when you switch on the optional Apple private cloud setting described below (off by default, iPhone only).
  • If you buy Pro (where offered), Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your card details.

How Ask writes answers

  • On iPhone with Apple Intelligence, written answers come from Apple’s on-device model (Apple Foundation Models). The app hands your question and a few matching passages to that model on the phone.
  • Apple’s private cloud (optional, off by default). In Settings → Ask you can switch on “Use Apple’s private cloud for harder questions” (iPhones with Apple Intelligence only). While it is off, nothing leaves your phone for Ask. While it is on, a harder question (for example about a whole document or several documents) together with the matching passages from your documents may be sent to Apple Private Cloud Compute to write the answer. Apple says Private Cloud Compute uses your data only to answer the request and does not store it or make it accessible to Apple. Such answers are labelled “Answered with Apple’s private cloud”. The data goes to Apple only, never to DawnShift or any other AI provider. Apple’s privacy policy applies.
  • On Android, written answers use the phone’s own on-device model where the phone supports it (Google’s Gemini Nano, through Android’s AICore system service). On other phones that can run it, you may be offered an optional “Smart answers” model to download. It is delivered by Google Play (not by a DawnShift server), the size is shown first, it downloads over Wi-Fi by default, it runs on your phone, and you can remove it in Settings. It is never downloaded without your choice. Phones that cannot run a model show the matching passages only.

Answers can be wrong. If a quote in an answer cannot be found word for word in your document, the app shows the matching passages instead.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Things you share. When you share, export, save or print, the destination you pick receives the file. Their privacy rules then apply.
  • Apple Private Cloud Compute, only if you switch it on (see above).
  • Google Play services on Android. The Android scan screen is Google’s document scanner, run by Google Play services. The first time it is used, Google Play services may download the scanner’s files. The optional “Smart answers” model is also downloaded by Google Play. Google, not this app, makes those downloads; we never receive your scans.
  • App stores. Apple and Google handle downloads, updates and any purchase under their own privacy policies.
  • Your phone’s backup. iCloud or Google backup may include the app’s stored documents, depending on your phone settings. Saved form details are excluded.

The app itself sends nothing to DawnShift. We have not measured what Apple’s or Google’s own system components send on their own; that is covered by their policies.

What we collect

Nothing from the app. We do not receive your documents, images, text, name, email, contacts or location. If you email us, we receive your email and use it only to reply.

Purchases

If your version offers Docwise Pro, it is bought and managed through the App Store or Google Play. The app asks the store whether you own Pro and keeps only a local “Pro unlocked” flag on your phone. We hold no payment details.

Deleting your data

Your documents never reach us, so we cannot see or delete them. In the app you can delete documents, saved signatures and saved details, remove a folder from search, and forget what the app learned about your folders. Uninstalling the app removes all app data from the phone. Files you shared stay where you sent them; delete them there. Phone backups are managed in your Apple or Google account.

Security

Files live in the app’s private storage, protected by your phone’s lock screen and encryption. App lock and the Private Vault ask the phone to confirm it is you; they rely on the phone’s protection rather than adding their own file encryption. Exported PDFs are not password-protected.

Children

Docwise is a general utility, not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Your rights

Wherever you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the GDPR), you can ask us what we hold about you at amakatsolutions@gmail.com. Our answer will be that we hold nothing from the app, other than any email you sent us.

Changes

If the app starts sending more data off the device, we will update this policy and the store privacy answers before that version ships, and change the effective date above.
