Bill Splitter

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

Bill Splitter keeps a record of shared expenses and works out who owes whom. It collects no data: everything you enter stays on your phone.

The short version

  • No account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no crash reporting. We run no server for this app.
  • It does not move money and never sees your bank account, card or UPI PIN. It is not a payment app.
  • Something leaves your phone only when you choose to share or save it.

What the app stores, and where

The groups, member names, expenses, payments and optional UPI IDs you type are stored in the app’s own storage on your phone. We never receive them.

Permissions

Bill Splitter does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. On Android it does not hold the internet permission, so it cannot open its own network connections; it holds only the Google Play billing and network-state permissions used by Google Play for the optional Pro purchase.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose

  • A summary (member names and amounts), a request card (a picture with a UPI QR code and the UPI ID you typed), a reminder (text with the UPI ID and a UPI payment link) or an exported file, sent through your share sheet to where you choose.
  • A backup file, written where you choose. It holds all your groups, member names, amounts and UPI IDs; keep it private.

What the other app or person does with what you share is outside this app.

Third parties

  • Google Play or the Apple App Store handle downloads and the optional one-time Pro purchase under their own terms. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Your phone’s backup (Google or iCloud) may include the app’s files, depending on your settings.

Deleting your data

Delete a group or expense in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete, and there is no account to close. Backup files you saved stay where you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version sends any data off your phone, we will update this policy and the store labels before that version ships, and change the effective date above.
