Common questions

My groups disappeared after changing phone.

Bill Splitter has no account, so we cannot bring them back. Before changing phone, open Settings → Back up all groups and save the file somewhere safe. On the new phone choose Restore from a backup. Restoring replaces every group on that phone, after you confirm.

How do I ask someone to pay me?

Open Balances, pick the payment, choose Request via UPI and Share request. The person paying scans the QR with their own UPI app. Bill Splitter cannot tell whether a payment was made, so use Record payment once the money arrives.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Only buying or restoring Pro needs the store.

What does Pro add?

The PDF report, the CSV export and spending insights, as one one-time purchase (not a subscription). Everything else is free.

Deleting your data

Delete a group in the app, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Restoring Pro and refunds

Sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account, open Settings and choose Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.
