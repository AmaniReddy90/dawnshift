Common questions

The hoop or the course will not appear.
AR needs light and a surface with some texture. Point the phone at a wall, table or floor that is not plain white or glossy, move the phone slowly from side to side for a few seconds, then tap when the guide shows the outline.

Does AR Arcade record or upload my camera?
No. The camera is used only to place the games in your room. Images are processed on your phone and are never recorded, saved or uploaded. The app makes no network requests.

Can I play without the camera?
Yes. Hoop Shot Classic and Chirp Run Classic play on the screen. If you said no to the camera, you can allow it later in your phone's Settings > AR Arcade.

Which phones support AR?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. On Android, a phone with Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?
On your phone only, so it is not moved to a new phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

The game feels too fast or the effects are too much.
Open Settings in the app and switch on Reduce motion, or turn sound and haptics off.

Is there a paid version?
Not yet. Version 1.0 sells nothing.
