AR Arcade

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

AR Arcade is a set of short games you play in your own room using your phone's camera (augmented reality), or on the screen without the camera. It is built so that nothing about you leaves your phone. There is no AR Arcade account and no DawnShift server.

The short version

  • The camera is used only to place the games in your room. Camera images are processed on your phone and are never recorded, saved or uploaded by the app.
  • Your profile (the display name you choose, XP, level, coins, best scores, daily-challenge streak, cosmetics) and your settings are saved on your phone only.
  • AR Arcade contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, makes no network requests and has no sign-in.
  • Version 1.0 sells nothing. If we add a paid option later, Apple or Google will take the payment and we will update this policy first.

What the app uses, and why

CameraTo find walls, tables and floors and place the games on them (Apple ARKit on iPhone, Google ARCore on Android).Processed on your phone. Not recorded, saved or uploaded.

Game progress and settingsXP, level, coins, bests, streak, cosmetics, sound, haptics and reduce-motion choices.Saved on your phone only.
Share button (where shown)Opens your phone's share sheet with a short text about your score.Goes only where you choose to send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising services. The game engines in the app (Flutter and Unity) run with their reporting features switched off.

Children

AR Arcade is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it all. We hold no copy and cannot recover it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the app.
