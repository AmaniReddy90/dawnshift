GST Invoice & Quote Maker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

GST Invoice & Quote Maker helps you make GST invoices and quotations on your phone. It works offline, with no account. What you type is stored on your phone and is not sent to us.

The short version

  • No account, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.
  • Information leaves your phone only when you choose to share or export something.
  • The only other contact is with Apple’s App Store or Google Play, to show Pro prices, buy Pro or restore it. We receive no purchase details.

What the app stores on your phone

WhatWhy
Your business profile (business name and state; optionally GSTIN, PAN, address, phone, email, website, UPI ID, bank details, notes and terms, logo, signature or stamp)Printed on the invoices and quotations you create.
Saved clients (name; optionally GSTIN, state, phone, address)So you can pick a client instead of retyping.
Saved items and services (name, price, GST rate, HSN/SAC)So you can add them quickly.
Invoices and quotations, payments you record, void status, invoice numberingSo you can reopen, number, share and audit your documents.
Reminder settingsSo payment reminders keep working.
Pro status (which product the store confirmed and the date)So Pro stays unlocked. It holds no name, email or payment detail.
Temporary copies of files you export or shareSo the share sheet can attach the file. Your phone may clear them; uninstalling removes them.

Customer names, GSTINs and addresses are information about your customers. The app keeps them only on your phone so you can issue documents to them.

Permissions and why

iPhone: the app does not ask for access to your photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts. If you choose a logo or restore a backup, your phone’s own picker shows and the app reads only the file you select. If you turn on payment reminders, your phone asks to allow notifications.

Android permissionWhy
Post notificationsOnly for payment reminders, if you turn them on.
Receive boot completedSo scheduled reminders are set again after your phone restarts.
VibrateLets a reminder vibrate if your settings allow.
Internet, network state and Google Play billingOnly so Google Play can show Pro prices, complete a purchase and restore it. Added by Google’s Play Billing software; the app has no network code of its own.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose

Each of these starts only when you tap it and opens your phone’s share sheet or file picker. The app or person you choose then holds the file under their own privacy rules.

  • Invoice or quotation PDF: your business details (including PAN and bank details if entered), your customer’s name and GSTIN, items and totals, and a UPI QR code if you entered a UPI ID.
  • Payment-request image and reminder message: amount, payer name, your business name and your UPI ID and payment link.
  • CSV report (Pro).
  • Backup file: all your data in one plain-text file. It is not encrypted; anyone who gets it can read it.

The UPI QR and link are built on your phone. The app does not process, see or confirm payments.

Third parties

  • Apple and Google handle downloads, updates and Pro purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • Your phone’s backup (iCloud or Google) may copy the app’s files, depending on your settings.

Notifications

Payment reminders are optional and off by default, and are scheduled on your phone only. By default a reminder shows the invoice number, client name and balance on your lock screen; switch off “Show client and amount in reminders” to show only the invoice number.

Deleting your data

There is no account and we hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on that phone. In the app you can delete quotations, clients, items and payment records. Issued invoices cannot be deleted, only voided, so your numbering has no gaps; to remove one completely, uninstall the app or restore a backup taken before it was created. Delete shared files and phone backups where they are kept.

Children

The app is a business tool for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not tax advice

The app calculates from the rates you enter. It does not give tax, accounting or legal advice and does not file returns or create e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting or sending data, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
