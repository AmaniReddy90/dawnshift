Email amakatsolutions@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few working days. Please do not send invoices, customer details, GSTINs, UPI IDs or bank details: we do not need them. Tell us your phone model, its system version and the app version (Settings, then About this app).

Common questions

Do I need an account or internet?

No account. The app works offline. Internet is needed only to show Pro prices, buy Pro or restore it.

Does the app file my GST return or make e-invoices?

No. It does not file returns, create e-invoices (IRN) or e-way bills, or connect to the GST portal.

Why did it split the tax into CGST and SGST, or use IGST?

Same-state supply is split into CGST and SGST; another state uses IGST. Check the place of supply and your business state in Settings.

I made a mistake on an invoice I already issued.

Use Void. A voided invoice stays on file and its number is never reused. You can undo a void.

Where do I find an exported PDF?

Android: in the folder you chose on the “save as” screen. iPhone: Files → On My iPhone → GST Invoice.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Backup & restore → Back up now, and keep the file somewhere safe. On the new phone, tap Restore from a file. The backup is not encrypted, so share it only with people you trust.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to delete everything it stored on that phone. We hold no copy. See the Privacy Policy.

Pro, cancelling and restoring

Cancel Pro Yearly on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Restore: Settings → Pro → Restore purchase with the same store account. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
