Common questions

Does PhotoOne upload my photos?

No. All analysis runs on your phone. There is no account and no server. On Android the app does not even have the internet permission.

I deleted a photo by mistake.

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted (iPhone) or Trash (Android 11 and later), where your phone keeps them for a limited time. Open your Photos or Gallery app to restore them. We cannot restore them for you.

Why does PhotoOne ask for contacts, calendar or usage access?

Only for the matching cleanups, and only when you use them. You can say no; the other features keep working. Before contacts or events are changed, PhotoOne saves a backup on your phone that you can restore.

Does Face analysis recognise people?

No. It only checks things like whether eyes are open, to suggest the best shot.

Deleting your data

Uninstall PhotoOne to remove everything it stored, including the Private vault. Move anything you want to keep out of the vault first. See the Privacy Policy.

Pro, cancelling and restoring

Use “Restore purchases” on the Pro screen with the same store account. Cancel the yearly plan on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
