Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Publisher: DawnShift · Contact: amakatsolutions@gmail.com

PhotoOne (“PhotoOne: Photo Cleaner”) helps you find similar, duplicate and low-quality photos and other clutter, and works entirely on your phone.

What we collect: nothing

PhotoOne has no servers, no account, no analytics and no advertising. On Android the app does not have the internet permission; on iPhone it makes no network requests of its own.

What the app uses, and why

Changes to your library

PhotoOne deletes only after you confirm in your phone’s own system dialog. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted (iPhone) or Trash (Android 11 and later). New photos it creates (Best Take, Space Saver) are saved to your library as new items; originals are removed only after you confirm.

iCloud Photos

On iPhone, PhotoOne works on your library through Apple’s Photos framework. If iCloud Photos is on, Apple’s system may download images it needs and syncs deletions to your other Apple devices. PhotoOne itself makes no connection to iCloud.

Private vault

Items you move to the vault are copied into PhotoOne’s private storage on your phone and protected by your phone’s Face ID, fingerprint or passcode. Your phone’s own backup may include them; PhotoOne never uploads them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your phone

Only what you share yourself. “Share without location” makes a temporary copy with the GPS location removed and hands it to your share sheet. Downloads and purchases go through Apple or Google under their own policies.

Purchases

PhotoOne Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google process the payment; PhotoOne only learns from your phone whether Pro is active. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Everything PhotoOne stores is on your phone and is removed when you delete the app. Photos you mark “Never suggest”, groups you keep and your settings are stored only on your phone. We hold nothing to delete.

Children

PhotoOne is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts sending data off the phone, we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version ships, and change the effective date above.
